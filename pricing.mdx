## Credits System

Wizzx uses a credit-based pricing system:
- **1000 credits = $1 USD**
- An accepted submission reserves credits. Successful tasks confirm the charge; failed tasks are refunded; `REVIEW_REQUIRED` tasks remain reserved for reconciliation.

<Note>
  `GET /pricing` is the source of truth for the rates of models documented here. Compatibility-only backend keys may be intentionally omitted from the public catalog below.
</Note>

## Video Generation

Video billing depends on the model. Veo 3.1 is fixed per generated video; other listed video models may bill per second or per request.

<Warning>
  Current gateway limitation: Seedance Pro Fast reaches its 480p default only when `resolution` is omitted; Seedance 1.5 Pro currently accepts explicit 720p only. The 480p catalog tiers remain listed for pricing transparency while the backend binding is being corrected.
</Warning>

## Cost Calculator

To calculate your cost:
1. Find the credits for your model and parameters above
2. Multiply by usage (e.g., seconds for video)
3. Divide by 1000 to get USD cost

**Example:** Generate a 10-second 720p video with Seedance Pro Fast
- Credits: 20 credits/s × 10s = 200 credits
- Cost: 200 ÷ 1000 = $0.20
