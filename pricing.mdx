## Credits System

Wizzx uses a credit-based pricing system:
- **1000 credits = $1 USD**
- An accepted submission reserves credits. Successful tasks confirm the charge; failed tasks are refunded; `REVIEW_REQUIRED` tasks remain reserved for reconciliation.

<Note>
  `GET /pricing` is the current source of truth. The tables below summarize the backend catalog and can change when model costs change.
</Note>

## Cost Calculator

To calculate your cost:
1. Find the credits for your model and parameters above
2. Multiply by usage (e.g., seconds for video)
3. Divide by 1000 to get USD cost

**Example:** Generate a 10-second 720p video with Seedance Pro Fast
- Credits: 20 credits/s × 10s = 200 credits
- Cost: 200 ÷ 1000 = $0.20
