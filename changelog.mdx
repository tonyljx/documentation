<Update label="September 30, 2026">

### New Model: GPT Image 2.5

**GPT Image 2.5 (OpenAI)**
- One model key, `gpt-image-2.5`, for text-to-image and image-to-image editing
- `version`: `flare` (default, faster) or `sunburst` (finer edits for product, ad, and multi-turn editing), same price
- Up to 16 reference images as HTTP(S) URLs or base64 data URIs (JPEG, PNG, WebP); reference images are not billed
- 11 aspect ratios including `auto`, and 1K / 2K / 4K output
- One image per request
- Pricing: 1K = 13, 2K = 21, 4K = 32 credits per image

**Music**

### Veo 3.1 Quality

- `veo3` now accepts `model: "veo3_quality"` at 720p, 1080p, or 4K for 1,924 / 1,924 / 2,885 credits per video.
- Quality supports 6 or 8 seconds and does not support `reference-to-video`. Fast and Lite are unchanged.

### Runway Gen-3 is offline

- `runway-gen3` no longer accepts new tasks. Submissions return HTTP 503 with error code `model_unavailable`, and the model is no longer listed in `GET /pricing`.
- Tasks accepted before the change still complete and settle normally, and you can keep polling them with `POST /api/v1/task/status`.
- For new video tasks, choose a model listed in `GET /pricing`, such as Veo 3.1, Kling AI, or Seedance.

<Update label="August 18, 2026">

### Veo 3.1 Lite and fixed per-video pricing

- Added `veo3_lite` alongside `veo3_fast` on the existing `veo3` endpoint.
- Fast and Lite support 720p, 1080p, and 4K with 4, 6, or 8-second output.
- Pricing is fixed per video by variant and resolution; duration does not multiply the charge.
- Lite costs 180/210/900 credits and Fast costs 360/390/1080 credits at 720p/1080p/4K.

</Update>
